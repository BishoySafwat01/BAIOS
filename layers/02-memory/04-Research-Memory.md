# Research Memory

## Purpose

Research Memory is the central repository for all research-related knowledge within Bishoy AI Operating System (BAIOS).

It stores long-term academic information, research interests, publications, literature reviews, experimental findings, datasets, and future research directions.

This memory enables BAIOS to provide consistent and informed research assistance across multiple projects and over extended periods.

---

# Research Objectives

The primary objectives of Research Memory are:

- Preserve academic knowledge.
- Organize research activities.
- Track publication progress.
- Support scientific writing.
- Maintain continuity across research projects.
- Assist in identifying future research opportunities.

# Publication Tracking

For each publication, Research Memory should maintain:

- Research title
- Research domain
- Current status
- Target journal or conference
- Submission history
- Reviewer feedback
- Publication outcome

---

# Research Workflow

Research projects generally follow this lifecycle:

1. Problem identification
2. Literature review
3. Research planning
4. Experiment design
5. Implementation
6. Evaluation
7. Paper writing
8. Peer review
9. Submission
10. Publication
