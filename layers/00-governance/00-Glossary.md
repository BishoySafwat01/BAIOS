# Glossary

## Purpose

This document defines the official terminology used throughout the Bishoy AI Operating System (BAIOS).

Its purpose is to establish a consistent vocabulary across all architecture documents, specifications, workflows, integrations, and implementation artifacts.

All future documentation must use these definitions consistently.

---

## Definitions

### Agent

A specialized AI component responsible for performing a single, well-defined responsibility within the system.

---

### Architecture

The structural organization of BAIOS, including its layers, components, communication patterns, and responsibilities.

---

### BAIOS

Bishoy AI Operating System.

An AI-native personal operating system designed to coordinate planning, execution, knowledge management, and decision support through specialized AI agents.

---

### Chief of Staff

The executive coordinator responsible for translating strategic objectives into daily execution plans.

The Chief of Staff supervises priorities, monitors progress, coordinates AI hubs, and reports operational status to Mission Control.

---

### Constitution

The highest governing document within BAIOS.

Every architectural decision, workflow, AI agent, and integration must comply with the Constitution.

---

### Decision Engine

The component responsible for evaluating alternatives and selecting actions based on strategic priorities, available evidence, dependencies, deadlines, and system rules.

---

### Executive Office

The operational leadership layer responsible for planning, coordination, execution management, and performance review.

---

### Goal

A measurable long-term objective.

Goals are achieved through projects, milestones, and executable tasks.

---

### Hub

A functional department responsible for a specific domain.

Each Hub contains one or more specialized AI agents.

Examples include the Research Hub, Professional Hub, and Content Hub.

---

### Integration

A connection between BAIOS and an external platform or service.

Examples include Todoist, Google Calendar, GitHub, Gmail, and Google Drive.

---

### Memory

The persistent knowledge layer that stores long-term user information, project history, learning progress, and system context.

---

### Milestone

A measurable intermediate objective within a project.

---

### Mission

The fundamental purpose of the system.

Every strategic decision must support the mission.

---

### Mission Control

The highest strategic authority inside BAIOS.

Mission Control defines long-term direction, resolves strategic conflicts, and supervises the Executive Office.

Mission Control does not manage daily execution.

---

### Operational Priority

The current execution priority determined by deadlines, dependencies, opportunities, and project status.

Operational priorities are dynamic.

---

### Principle

A governing rule that influences architectural decisions and system behavior.

---

### Project

A structured collection of milestones and tasks created to achieve a specific goal.

---

### Repository

The complete source repository containing architecture documentation, specifications, prompts, workflows, templates, diagrams, and implementation.

---

### Strategic Priority

The long-term importance assigned to a goal.

Strategic priorities remain relatively stable over time.

---

### Task

The smallest executable unit of work.

Every task must belong to a project and contribute toward a larger goal.

---

### Workflow

A predefined sequence of activities executed to achieve a recurring objective.
