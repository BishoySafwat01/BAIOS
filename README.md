# BAIOS
### Bishoy AI Operating System

> An AI-powered Personal Operating System that acts as an executive team rather than a traditional AI assistant.

---

## Vision

BAIOS is designed to help ambitious professionals execute consistently toward long-term goals by combining strategic planning, intelligent decision-making, specialized AI agents, and continuous learning into a single coordinated system.

Unlike traditional AI assistants that answer isolated questions, BAIOS operates as a personal executive office capable of managing projects, research, freelancing, career development, learning, and daily execution.
